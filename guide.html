Pet Café Merge gameplay guide

Pet Café Merge is a cat café merge puzzle for iOS. This official guide explains what you do in the game, without walkthroughs or spoiler-heavy tips.

A merge coffee game with cats

You run a tiny cat café. In each level, you merge matching coffee items into something better, from beans and espresso toward the café’s Signature Brew. Levels are short: each one gives you a goal and a limited number of moves.

To merge, tap one item, then tap another item of the same kind. That is the basic action of the puzzle. The rest of the game is about the café around those levels — the town, the cats, and the spaces you unlock as you play.

Cat Town: 50 levels and 5 districts

Cat Town is the map that holds the puzzle levels. There are 50 levels, arranged along a path through 5 districts. Café cats can be seen around the map as you go. The aim is a relaxed route through the town, not a checklist of hidden solutions.

Six café cats and dress-up

The café is home to six cats: Maple, Pepper, Mochi, Biscuit, Peanut, and Luna. At Home, they live together. You can visit them, and you can dress them up. Dress-up changes how a cat looks; it does not change the core merge rules.

You can also put a cat on duty so it can help during a level. Help from cats is part of normal play, not something you have to buy.

Shop, languages, and playing on iOS

Coins you earn in levels can be spent in the shop on themes and decor. Pet Café Merge is an iOS game, with English and Simplified Chinese. No account is required, and your progress is saved on your device.

Levels can be played without an internet connection. Optional video ads, in-app purchases, and content updates need a connection.
