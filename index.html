Pet Café Merge

A cat café merge puzzle for iOS. Merge coffee, run a tiny café, and spend time with six café cats.

View on the App Store

The game is available in English and Simplified Chinese.

What’s in the game

  • Merge matching coffee items in short levels to brew better drinks.
  • Explore Cat Town: 50 levels across 5 districts.
  • Meet six café cats — Maple, Pepper, Mochi, Biscuit, Peanut, and Luna — and dress them up.
  • Visit Home, decorate your shop, and play at your own pace.

No account is required. Levels can be played without an internet connection. Optional video ads, purchases, and content updates need a connection.

Read the gameplay guide for a clearer picture of how the café, the cats, and the merge levels fit together.
