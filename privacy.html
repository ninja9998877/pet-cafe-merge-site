Pet Café Merge – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026 (version 1.0.5)

This policy explains how the mobile game Pet Café Merge ("the App") handles information. The App has no user accounts and no servers of ours: your game progress stays on your device. Version 1.0.3 adds optional rewarded ads, an optional in-app purchase and anonymous usage analytics, described below.

Summary

  • No account, no sign-in, no name, e-mail, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location is collected.
  • Rewarded video ads (Google AdMob) appear only when you tap "Watch ×2". The App asks for your permission first (Apple's tracking prompt and, where required, Google's consent form).
  • Anonymous usage analytics (Google Firebase Analytics) tell us which levels are too hard or which screens are used.
  • One optional in-app purchase (Supporter Pack, non-consumable) is handled by Apple.
  • If you own the Supporter Pack, the App shows no ads and does not ask for tracking permission or ad consent.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, items, coins, settings, daily reward and offline tips state, purchase status cache) is saved locally on your device only and we cannot access it. Delete it at any time with "Clear Save" in Settings or by deleting the App. Test (TestFlight / internal) builds additionally keep a small text file analytics.log with the same event names listed below, plus a test-only memory reading (mem_shop); it stays on the tester's device and is not uploaded.

Microphone – "Meow to call"

Microphone: if you allow it, the game measures how loud you are for a few seconds when you tap "Call" in the shop, to detect a meow. This happens only on your device. We do not record, store or upload audio and do not use speech recognition. Our analytics only count how often the feature is used and whether the cat heard you.

The microphone is optional. If you decline access, the same button calls the cat without the microphone. You can turn the feature off in Settings → Meow to call, or turn off microphone access in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

Advertising – Google AdMob

The App shows rewarded video ads only (no banners, no interstitials) using Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). An ad is requested only after you tap "Watch ×2" or open a screen that offers it. Before the first ad request:

  • App Tracking Transparency (ATT). iOS asks whether the App may track you across other companies' apps and websites. The advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow it. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads still work, but without the IDFA.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Where the law requires it (for example the EEA and the UK) Google's consent form is shown and you can accept or decline personalised ads.

AdMob and Google may process device and advertising identifiers, IP address (approximate location), ad interactions and diagnostics to deliver, measure and secure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from apps that use AdMob. We receive no personal data from AdMob.

Analytics – Google Firebase Analytics

The App uses Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played. It does not include any personal information. Events (name – parameters):

Firebase also automatically collects technical information such as a random app-instance identifier, device model, operating system and app version, language, and approximate region derived from the IP address. We do not combine it with any name or account, and we do not use it for advertising. See Firebase privacy information and Google's Privacy Policy.

In-app purchase – Apple and Unity IAP

The optional Supporter Pack is a one-time (non-consumable) purchase processed by Apple through the App Store; we never see your payment details. The App uses Unity In-App Purchasing to talk to StoreKit and check or restore your purchase ("Restore Purchases" in Settings). Unity may receive purchase transaction information for this purpose; see Unity's Privacy Policy. Apple's terms: Apple Privacy Policy.

Retention

Game progress stays on your device until you clear it or delete the App. Firebase Analytics event data is retained by Google for 2 months (Firebase's default event-data retention) and then deleted; we hold no other server-side data about you. Advertising data is kept by Google under its own policy.

Your choices (opt-out)

  • Anonymous analytics (in the App): Settings → Anonymous analytics switch. It is on by default; switch it off at any time. When off, the App stops Firebase Analytics collection and stops writing the local log immediately and on every later start. Your choice is saved on your device and also applies to Supporter Pack owners.
  • Privacy options (in the App, EU / UK): where Google's consent rules require it, Settings shows a Privacy options button that reopens Google's consent form so you can change your ad consent at any time. The button does not appear in other regions.
  • Tracking (iOS): iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → switch Pet Café Merge off (or switch "Allow Apps to Request to Track" off for all apps). Without permission the App never uses the advertising identifier.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or limit it in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising (turn Personalized Ads off) and, on supported iOS versions, Tracking settings; deleting and reinstalling the App also starts a new app-instance identifier.
  • Ads: watching a rewarded ad is always optional. Owning the Supporter Pack removes all ads and ad prompts.
  • Your data: we do not hold personal data that we can look up, export or delete for you. For data held by Google or Apple, use their privacy controls, or e-mail us and we will help.

Children's privacy

Pet Café Merge is a general-audience game (App Store age rating 4+). It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Ads are shown only after the permission steps above; the App has no chat, no social features and no accounts. If you believe a child's personal information has reached us, e-mail us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.

萌宠咖啡馆（Pet Café Merge）隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 18 日（1.0.5 版）

本政策说明手机游戏《Pet Café Merge》（“本应用”）如何处理信息。本应用没有用户账号，也没有我们自己的服务器：你的游戏进度只保存在你的设备上。1.0.3 版新增了可选的激励视频广告、一项可选的应用内购买和匿名的使用统计，详见下文。

要点

  • 不需要账号或登录，不收集姓名、邮箱、电话、通讯录、照片或精确位置。
  • 只有点击「看视频 ×2」时才会出现激励视频广告（Google AdMob），事先会征求你的许可（苹果的跟踪授权弹窗，以及法律要求地区的 Google 同意表单）。
  • 匿名使用统计（Google Firebase Analytics）帮助我们了解哪些关卡太难、哪些界面常用。
  • 一项可选的应用内购买（支持包，非消耗型）由苹果处理。
  • 拥有支持包的玩家看不到广告，也不会被询问跟踪授权或广告同意。

保存在你设备上的数据

游戏进度（关卡、物品、金币、设置、每日奖励与离线小费状态、购买状态缓存）只保存在你的设备本地，我们无法访问。你可以随时在「设置」里点「清除存档」或删除本应用来清除。测试版（TestFlight / 内部测试）还会在设备上保留一个文本文件 analytics.log，内容是下表中的同名事件，外加一条只在测试版记录的内存读数（mem_shop），只留在测试者的设备上，不会上传。

麦克风 – 声音叫猫

麦克风：如果你允许，在小店里点「叫猫」后的几秒内，游戏会检测你的声音有多大，用来判断你有没有学猫叫。这只在你的设备上进行。我们不录音、不保存、不上传音频，也不使用语音识别。我们的统计只记录这个功能的使用次数，以及猫咪有没有听到你。

麦克风是可选的。如果你拒绝授权，同一个按钮不用麦克风也能叫猫。你可以在「设置」→「声音叫猫」里关闭这个功能，或在 iOS「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 麦克风」里关闭本应用的麦克风权限。

广告 – Google AdMob

本应用只展示激励视频广告（没有横幅、没有插屏），使用 Google AdMob（Google Mobile Ads SDK）。只有在你点击「看视频 ×2」或打开提供该选项的界面后才会请求广告。首次请求广告前：

  • App 跟踪透明度（ATT）。iOS 会询问是否允许本应用跨其他公司的 App 和网站跟踪你。只有你允许，才会使用广告标识符（IDFA）。若选择「要求 App 不跟踪」，广告仍可观看，只是不使用 IDFA。
  • Google 用户消息平台（UMP）。在法律要求的地区（如欧洲经济区、英国）会显示 Google 的同意表单，你可以接受或拒绝个性化广告。

AdMob 与 Google 可能处理设备和广告标识符、IP 地址（大致位置）、广告互动和诊断信息，用于投放、衡量和保护广告安全。详见 Google 隐私权政策及Google 如何使用使用 AdMob 的应用中的数据。我们不会从 AdMob 收到任何个人数据。

使用统计 – Google Firebase Analytics

本应用使用 Firebase Analytics 了解游戏的玩法，不包含任何个人信息。事件（名称 – 参数）：

事件参数
app_open、session_length本次使用的秒数
level_start、level_win、level_fail、level_quit关卡号、剩余步数、星级；level_fail 另带失败原因（moves 步数用完 / stuck 无路可走）
ad_request、ad_shown、ad_reward、ad_fail、ad_no_fill广告位（每日奖励 / 离线小费）
daily_claim、offline_claim第几天（1–7）、是否翻倍
iap_view、iap_purchase、iap_cancel、iap_restore、iap_fail入口、原因
walkin_serve、tutorial_step、shop_buy、cat_treat步骤 / 物品 / 猫咪编号
undo_use关卡号、被撤销的操作（move 移动、merge 合成、ingredient 放食材、discard 丢弃、sell 出售、add_bean 加咖啡豆、clear_lowest 清理最低级、walk_in 散客）、剩余可撤销步数、退回的步数
call_cat猫咪编号（cap 时不发送）、结果（heard 听到、button 按钮、button_fallback 无麦克风改用按钮、timeout 超时、interrupted 被打断、cooldown 冷却中、cap 达到每日上限）——不含音频、音量数值或文字

Firebase 还会自动收集技术信息，如随机的应用实例标识符、设备型号、系统和应用版本、语言，以及根据 IP 推断的大致地区。我们不会把这些信息与任何姓名或账号关联，也不用于广告。详见 Firebase 隐私信息和 Google 隐私权政策。

应用内购买 – Apple 与 Unity IAP

可选的支持包是一次性（非消耗型）购买，由苹果通过 App Store 处理，我们看不到你的支付信息。本应用通过 Unity In-App Purchasing 与 StoreKit 通信，用于检查和恢复购买（「设置」里的「恢复购买」）。Unity 可能为此收到购买交易信息，详见 Unity 隐私政策。苹果条款：Apple 隐私政策。

保留期限

游戏进度在你清除存档或删除本应用之前一直保存在设备上。Google 对 Firebase Analytics 事件数据保留 2 个月（Firebase 默认的事件数据保留期），之后删除；我们没有其他关于你的服务器端数据。广告数据由 Google 按其自身政策保留。

你的选择（退出方式）

  • 匿名统计（应用内）：「设置」→「匿名统计」开关，默认开启，可随时关闭。关闭后，本应用立即并在以后每次启动时停止 Firebase Analytics 的数据收集，也不再写入本地日志。你的选择保存在设备上，拥有支持包的玩家同样可用。
  • 隐私选项（应用内，欧盟 / 英国）：在 Google 同意规则要求的地区，「设置」里会出现「隐私选项」按钮，可随时重新打开 Google 同意表单修改广告同意；其他地区不会显示该按钮。
  • 跟踪（iOS）：iOS「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪」里关闭 Pet Café Merge（或关闭「允许 App 请求跟踪」）。未经许可，本应用不会使用广告标识符。
  • 广告标识符：可在 iOS「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → Apple 广告」里关闭个性化广告来重置或限制；删除并重装本应用也会生成新的应用实例标识符。
  • 广告：观看激励广告始终是自愿的；拥有支持包即可去除全部广告和相关提示。
  • 你的数据：我们没有可供查询、导出或删除的个人数据；Google 或苹果持有的数据请使用它们的隐私设置，或发邮件给我们，我们会协助。

儿童隐私

《Pet Café Merge》是面向一般受众的游戏（App Store 年龄分级 4+），不是面向 13 岁以下儿童的产品，也不会有意收集任何人（包括儿童）的个人信息。广告仅在上述许可步骤之后展示；本应用没有聊天、社交功能或账号。如果你认为某位儿童的个人信息已到达我们这里，请发邮件，我们会将其删除。

政策变更

如果未来版本改变了数据处理方式，我们会在该版本发布前更新本页面和上方日期。
