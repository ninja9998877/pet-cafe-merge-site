Pet Café Merge – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Pet Café Merge ("the App") handles information.

We do not collect personal data

The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. It has no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. The App does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, items, settings) is saved locally on your device only. It never leaves your device and we cannot access it. You can delete it at any time with "Clear Save" in the App's Settings or by deleting the App.

Children's privacy

Because the App collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the App changes how data is handled, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.
