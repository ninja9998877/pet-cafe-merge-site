Pet Café Merge – Privacy Policy

Effective: October 21, 2026 (version 1.1.0)

This policy explains how the mobile game Pet Café Merge ("the App") handles information. The App has no user accounts and no servers of ours: your game progress stays on your device. The App is developed by Kachanali Studio (developer: 刘兆楠 Liu Zhaonan) ("we").

Summary

  • No account, no sign-in. We do not collect your name, e-mail, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Optional rewarded video ads (Google AdMob) play only when you tap "Watch ×2". The App asks your permission first (Apple's tracking prompt and, where required, Google's consent form).
  • Anonymous usage analytics (Google Firebase Analytics) show us how the game is played, for example which levels are too hard. You can switch it off in Settings.
  • Microphone (optional): only checks how loud you are when you call a cat. Nothing is recorded, saved or uploaded.
  • Optional in-app purchases (the Supporter Pack and 6 cosmetic cat outfits) are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • Optional local reminders (tip jar full) are created on your device; nothing is sent to us.
  • If you own the Supporter Pack, the App shows no ads and does not ask for tracking permission or ad consent.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, items, coins, cats, hearts, outfits, decor, settings, daily reward and tip-jar state, purchase status cache) is saved locally on your device only, and we cannot access it. Delete it at any time with "Clear Save" in Settings or by deleting the App. Test (TestFlight / internal) builds additionally keep a small local log of the same event names (plus a test-only memory reading); it stays on the tester's device and is not uploaded.

Microphone – "Meow to call"

If you allow it, when you tap "Call" in Home the App measures how loud you are for a few seconds to detect a meow. This happens only on your device. We do not record, store or upload audio and do not use speech recognition. Analytics only count how often the feature is used and whether the cat heard you. The microphone is optional. If you decline access, the same button calls the cat without it. Turn the feature off in Settings → Meow to call, or turn off microphone access in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

Notifications

If you tap "Sure" on the in-game card and then allow notifications, the App schedules a local reminder on your device when the tip jar is full (at most one a day, never between 22:00 and 09:00). No push service or notification token is used. Turn it off in iOS Settings → Notifications.

Advertising – Google AdMob

The App shows rewarded video ads only (no banners, no interstitials) using Google AdMob. An ad is requested only after you tap "Watch ×2" or open a screen that offers it, never at launch. Before the first ad request: - App Tracking Transparency (ATT). iOS asks whether the App may track you across other companies' apps and websites. The advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow it. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads still work without the IDFA. - Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Where the law requires it (for example the EEA and the UK), Google's consent form is shown, and you can accept or decline personalised ads. AdMob and Google may process device and advertising identifiers, IP address (approximate location), ad interactions and diagnostics to deliver, measure and secure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services". We receive no personal data from AdMob.

Analytics – Google Firebase Analytics

The App uses Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played. Events contain gameplay information only (no name, account, audio or text):

Firebase also automatically collects technical information such as a random app-instance identifier, device model, operating system and app version, language, and approximate region derived from the IP address. We do not combine it with any name or account and do not use it for advertising. See Firebase's privacy information and Google's Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases – Apple and Unity IAP

The optional Supporter Pack and the 6 cat outfits (Little Postie, Tiny Detective, Little Artist, Little Lion, Little Firefighter, Bath Time) are one-time, non-consumable purchases processed by Apple through the App Store. Outfits change a cat's looks only. We never receive your card or payment details. The App uses Unity In-App Purchasing to talk to StoreKit and to check or restore purchases ("Restore Purchases" in Settings); Unity may receive transaction information for this purpose (see Unity's Privacy Policy). Purchase events listed above are sent to Firebase without payment details.

Retention and deletion

  • Game progress stays on your device until you use "Clear Save" or delete the App.
  • Firebase Analytics event data is kept by Google for TODO: 2 months (confirm console setting) and then deleted. We hold no other server-side data about you.
  • Advertising data is kept by Google under its own policy.
  • We cannot look up data by person because there are no accounts. To ask about deletion of analytics data linked to your app-instance identifier, e-mail us (see Contact); deleting and reinstalling the App also starts a new identifier.

Your choices

  • Anonymous analytics: Settings → Anonymous analytics (on by default). When off, the App stops Firebase Analytics collection immediately and on every later start.
  • Privacy options (EU / UK): where Google's rules require it, Settings shows "Privacy options" to reopen the consent form.
  • Tracking: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Without permission the App never uses the advertising identifier.
  • Ads are always optional; the Supporter Pack removes them.

Children's privacy

Pet Café Merge is a general-audience game (App Store age rating 4+). It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The App has no chat, no social features and no accounts. If you believe a child's personal information has reached us, e-mail us and we will help remove it.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.

Pet Café Merge 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 21 日（1.1.0 版）

本政策说明手机游戏《Pet Café Merge》（“本应用”）如何处理信息。本应用没有用户账号，也没有我们自己的服务器：你的游戏进度只保存在你的设备上。本应用由咔嚓纳利工作室 Kachanali Studio（开发者：刘兆楠）开发（“我们”）。

要点

  • 不需要账号或登录，不收集姓名、邮箱、电话、通讯录、照片或精确位置。
  • 可选的激励视频广告（Google AdMob）只在你点「看视频 ×2」时播放，事先会征求你的许可（苹果的跟踪授权弹窗，以及法律要求地区的 Google 同意表单）。
  • 匿名使用统计（Google Firebase Analytics）帮助我们了解游戏怎么被玩，比如哪些关卡太难。可在「设置」里关闭。
  • 麦克风（可选）：只在你叫猫时检测音量，不录音、不保存、不上传。
  • 可选的应用内购买（支持包和 6 套猫咪衣服）由苹果处理，我们看不到你的付款信息。
  • 可选的本地提醒（小费罐满了）只在你的设备上生成，不会发给我们。
  • 拥有支持包的玩家看不到广告，也不会被询问跟踪授权或广告同意。

保存在你设备上的数据

游戏进度（关卡、物品、金币、猫咪、好感、衣服、摆件、设置、每日奖励与小费罐状态、购买状态缓存）只保存在你的设备本地，我们无法访问。可随时在「设置」里点「清除存档」或删除本应用来清除。测试版（TestFlight / 内部测试）还会在设备上保留一份同名事件的本地日志（外加一条测试版专用的内存读数），只留在测试者的设备上，不会上传。

麦克风 – 声音叫猫

如果你允许，在「家」里点「叫猫」后的几秒内，游戏会检测你的声音有多大，用来判断你有没有学猫叫。这只在你的设备上进行。我们不录音、不保存、不上传音频，也不使用语音识别。统计只记录这个功能的使用次数，以及猫咪有没有听到你。 麦克风是可选的。拒绝授权后，同一个按钮不用麦克风也能叫猫。可在「设置」→「声音叫猫」里关闭，或在 iOS「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 麦克风」里关闭本应用的权限。

通知

如果你在游戏内说明卡上点「好」并允许通知，小费罐存满时本应用会在设备上安排一条本地提醒（每天最多一条，22:00–09:00 不发）。不使用推送服务，也不上传通知令牌。可在 iOS「设置 → 通知」里关闭。

广告 – Google AdMob

本应用只展示激励视频广告（没有横幅、没有插屏），使用 Google AdMob。只有你点「看视频 ×2」或打开提供该选项的界面后才会请求广告，启动时不会请求。首次请求广告前： - App 跟踪透明度（ATT）。 iOS 会询问是否允许本应用跨其他公司的 App 和网站跟踪你。只有你允许，才会使用广告标识符（IDFA）。选择「要求 App 不跟踪」后广告照样能看，只是不使用 IDFA。 - Google 用户消息平台（UMP）。 在法律要求的地区（如欧洲经济区、英国）会显示 Google 的同意表单，你可以接受或拒绝个性化广告。 AdMob 与 Google 可能处理设备和广告标识符、IP 地址（大致位置）、广告互动和诊断信息，用于投放、衡量和保护广告安全。详见 Google 隐私权政策。我们不会从 AdMob 收到任何个人数据。

使用统计 – Google Firebase Analytics

本应用使用 Firebase Analytics 了解游戏的玩法。事件只含游戏信息（不含姓名、账号、音频或文字）：关卡开始 / 过关 / 失败 / 退出（关卡号、剩余步数、星级、失败原因），猫咪技能发动（猫咪编号、技能等级），引导步骤，地图街区与页签，家里点猫、喂零食、叫猫结果（不含音频或音量），换装与衣服购买结果，支持包购买 / 恢复，广告请求与结果，每日奖励与离线小费领取，撤销，存档升级结果，以及 first_open（自动）、app_open 和单次使用时长。完整事件名见英文版表格。 Firebase 还会自动收集技术信息，例如随机的应用实例标识符、设备型号、系统和应用版本、语言，以及由 IP 推算的大致地区。我们不会把这些信息和任何姓名或账号关联，也不用于广告。详见 Firebase 隐私说明和 Google 隐私权政策。

应用内购买 – Apple 与 Unity IAP

可选的支持包和 6 套猫咪衣服（小邮差、小侦探、小画家、小狮子、小消防员、泡澡时光）都是一次性、非消耗型购买，由苹果通过 App Store 处理。衣服只改变猫咪外观。我们不会收到你的银行卡或付款信息。本应用通过 Unity In-App Purchasing 与 StoreKit 通信，用于查询和恢复购买（「设置」里的「恢复购买」）；Unity 可能为此收到交易信息（见 Unity 隐私政策）。上表中的购买事件发送到 Firebase 时不含付款信息。

保存期限与删除

  • 游戏进度留在你的设备上，直到你「清除存档」或删除本应用。
  • Firebase 统计事件数据由 Google 保存 TODO：2 个月（待在控制台确认）后删除。我们没有其他关于你的服务器数据。
  • 广告数据由 Google 按其政策保存。
  • 因为没有账号，我们无法按个人查找数据。如需删除与你的应用实例标识符相关的统计数据，请发邮件给我们（见「联系我们」）；删除并重装本应用也会生成新的标识符。

你的选择

  • 匿名统计：「设置」→「匿名统计」（默认开启）。关闭后，本应用立即并在之后每次启动时停止 Firebase 统计。
  • 隐私选项（欧盟 / 英国）： 在 Google 规则要求的地区，「设置」里有「隐私选项」，可重新打开同意表单。
  • 跟踪： iOS「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪」。未经许可，本应用从不使用广告标识符。
  • 广告始终可选；支持包会去掉广告。

儿童隐私

Pet Café Merge 是面向所有年龄的游戏（App Store 分级 4+），不以 13 岁以下儿童为对象，也不会有意收集任何人（包括儿童）的个人信息。本应用没有聊天、社交功能和账号。如果你认为有儿童的个人信息到了我们这里，请发邮件给我们，我们会协助删除。

政策变更

如果以后的版本改变数据处理方式，我们会在该版本发布前更新本页和上面的日期。
