FAQ

How do I merge? Tap one item, then tap another item of the same kind to merge them into the next level.

Where is my progress saved? Progress is saved only on your device. Deleting the app or using "Clear Save" in Settings will erase it.

Does the game need an internet connection? No. The game works fully offline.
