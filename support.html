FAQ

How do I merge? Tap one item, then tap another item of the same kind to merge them into the next level.

Where is my progress saved? Progress is saved only on your device. Deleting the app or using “Clear Save” in Settings will erase it.

Does the game need an internet connection? Levels can be played without an internet connection. Optional video ads, in-app purchases, and content updates need a connection.

For a short overview of the café, Cat Town, and the cats, see the gameplay guide. The game is on the App Store.
